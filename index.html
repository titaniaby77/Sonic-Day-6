After Solaris // Canon Story

The true Solaris remains.

After Super Sonic, Super Shadow and Super Silver defeat Solaris, the others celebrate — but Sonic and Elise are drawn toward a tiny white flame.

This is Solaris at its origin: the Flame of Hope, before the Solaris Project ever caused the disaster.

Elise understands the cost. If she extinguishes it, Solaris will never exist — but the events that brought her and Sonic together will also never happen.

Flame of Hope // Canon Ending

The timeline is rewritten.

Elise hesitates because extinguishing Solaris means losing the history she shared with Sonic. Sonic encourages her to smile, and she finally blows out the tiny flame.

Solaris, Iblis and Mephiles are erased from history. Time resets to the Festival of the Sun — but this time Eggman's attack never happens.

During the peaceful festival, Sonic races through Soleanna. The wind from his speed catches Elise's feather. For a moment she feels as if someone familiar is calling to her, though she cannot explain why.

Sonic watches from a rooftop as the feather rises into the night sky. The original game's story ends there.
